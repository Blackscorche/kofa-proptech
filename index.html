Renting in three steps

You never need to send money before you have seen the house.

  1. Search homes

    Browse photos, prices and locations. Each home was published by a registered agent, and the agent's ID is on the listing.

  2. Inspect in person

    Request an inspection from the agent. Walk through the home and ask your questions before you decide.

  3. Pay through Kofa

    If you like the home, agree the terms and pay on the platform. Kofa records the landlord's rent and the agent's commission separately.

What keeps scams out

Landlord uploads stay private

Only registered agents can see what landlords upload. The public sees a home only after an agent publishes it.

A named agent on every listing

Each listing is tied to the agent who published it, so someone is always accountable for what you read.

Payments on the record

Rent and agent commission are paid through the platform and recorded as two separate amounts.

Own a house to rent out?
